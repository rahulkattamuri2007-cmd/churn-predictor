========================================================
PROJECT NAME: Customer Churn Predictor with Explainability
========================================================

SUMMARY:
A machine learning web app that predicts whether a customer is likely
to leave (churn) a subscription-based service, using their account
details (tenure, contract type, monthly charges, etc.). The project
goes beyond a basic classifier by adding SHAP explainability (so you
can show WHY the model made a prediction) and a live Streamlit app
where anyone can input customer details and get an instant prediction.

Tech stack: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, SHAP, Streamlit, matplotlib
IDE: Antigravity
Dataset: Telco Customer Churn (Kaggle)
Estimated time: 4-6 hours (one weekend)

Why this is GitHub-worthy:
- Real business problem (churn prevention)
- Model comparison (not just one algorithm)
- Explainability (SHAP) - shows maturity beyond tutorial-level work
- Deployed, interactive app - not just a notebook
- Clear documentation
